# Changelog

All notable changes to PrimeCrest Construction Management are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased] - PrimeCrest rebrand

First release line of **PrimeCrest Construction Management** by **Spyder Solutions**, rebased
on the upstream OpenConstructionERP 18.9.0 codebase and refocused on the Pakistan
construction market.

### Changed

- Product renamed to PrimeCrest Construction Management (short: PrimeCrest), developed by
  Spyder Solutions. User-facing branding, documentation, contact details
  (support@primecrest.pk, https://primecrest.pk, https://docs.primecrest.pk) and repository
  links (https://github.com/spyderps05/PrimeCrestConstMgmt) updated throughout.
- Defaults oriented to Pakistan: PKR currency, federal (FBR / ICT) and provincial sales tax on
  services (PRA Punjab, SRB Sindh, KPRA Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, BRA Balochistan), PEC contractor
  categories, Urdu and English interface.
- Demo data refocused on projects in Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad.
- README rewritten as a product README organised by role (Owner / Director, Project Manager,
  Site Engineer, QS / Estimator, Procurement, Accounts).

### Removed

- Upstream community, sponsorship, consulting and commercial-licence marketing, upstream
  social links (Telegram, YouTube, LinkedIn) and upstream contact addresses from the
  documentation.

### Licensing

- PrimeCrest remains under AGPL-3.0. © 2026 Spyder Solutions. PrimeCrest is based on
  OpenConstructionERP © Artem Boiko / DataDrivenConstruction, licensed under AGPL-3.0.
  Upstream copyright headers in source files, `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, `COPYRIGHT` and
  `docs/legal/` are retained unchanged.
